Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

1. Introduction

MailArmour is committed to protecting your security and privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we process the data parsed by the MailArmour Chrome extension when checking for email safety indicators.

2. Data We Process

MailArmour analyzes email metadata (such as routing headers, links, and subject lines) inside your browser client. The email text content processed by MailArmour is evaluated in real-time and immediately discarded. We do not store, log, or aggregate the content of your scanned emails.

3. Local Statistics

Your scan history, weekly statistics, domain whitelist configurations, and total emails scanned counts are stored entirely locally on your device using the browser's storage API. This data is never uploaded to our servers and remains completely under your control.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Paid Pro plan subscriptions are processed through Gumroad. Credit card numbers, transaction details, and payment information are handled securely under Gumroad's privacy policies. We do not store financial payment information on our servers.
